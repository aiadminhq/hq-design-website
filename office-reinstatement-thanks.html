Office Reinstatement

Got it! We'll be in touch soon.

Thank you. There's nothing else you need to do; just look out for our call or email.

Your reference number

Quote this number when you contact us and we can find your details faster.

What happens next

  1. We contact youBy the phone number or email you gave us, to get a rough picture of your situation and your lease end date.
  2. We arrange a site surveyAt a time that suits you, we visit the office to measure, take photos, and confirm what goes and what stays.
  3. We send a quote and schedulePriced on floor area and scope, with how many days the work will take.

Documents you can get ready

Dig these out if you have them. If not, that's fine; we'll go through it with you when we get in touch.

  • Photos of the space as it is nowPhone photos are fine, a few of each area.
  • Photos or handover records from when you moved inThese show what the space “originally” looked like.
  • The landlord's reinstatement list, or the reinstatement clause in your leaseWhat the landlord wants removed and what they want put back. If it was only agreed verbally, tell us that too.
  • Original drawingsAs-built drawings or interior fit-out drawings.
  • The building's rules for worksPermitted working hours, noise limits, and rules for waste removal and loading.
