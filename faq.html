Frequently asked questions

For procurement, corporate real estate, and international design PMs evaluating Taiwan commercial fit-out.

Short, citable answers based on confirmed live-site facts. For Local Partner scope detail see Local Partner; to start a conversation use Contact.

Company & licence

Who is HQ Design — legal name and license?

HQ Design CO., LTD. (Chinese legal name: 惠強室內裝修股份有限公司; brand: HQ Design; Tax ID / UBN 89956251) is a Taipei-registered interior alteration / interior renovation contractor (室內裝修業), founded in 1995. We have 31+ years of experience and 1,600+ completed commercial projects. Our office is at 9F, No. 135 Jingmao 2nd Rd, Nangang, Taipei.

Engagement models

Design & Build vs Local Partner — which do we need?

Choose Design & Build when you want one turnkey contract for design and construction under one in-house team and one PM — from briefing through handover.

Choose Local Partner when an overseas or captive design studio keeps design authorship and the client relationship; HQ Design then handles Taiwan permits, compliance, procurement and site supervision. Many international design PMs and CRE teams use the Local Partner path. See Local Partner and Services.

Can our overseas design studio keep design authorship?

Yes. Under the Local Partner model we do not take over design authorship. Your studio keeps creative control and the client relationship. HQ Design produces local permit and tender drawings and runs Taiwan landlord and statutory coordination under your design lead.

Can we engage HQ for permits only while design stays with us?

Yes, after assessment. Permits and compliance can be purchased standalone even when another firm designs and builds. We also offer build-only and full local execution (permits, procurement and site) while you keep design lead. Tell us which model you need when you enquire.

Taiwan permits & delivery

Who handles Taiwan interior renovation, fire and landlord permits?

HQ Design coordinates interior renovation filings, related fire submissions, and landlord / building-management requirements as applicable to each project. Scope is assessed with the proposal — we do not publish a fixed always-in-scope permit matrix. Permit submissions are stamped by a registered interior-renovation professional on our team.

Can you support zero-downtime or occupied-building fit-outs?

Yes at a programme level. Our site positioning includes Zero Downtime / turnkey delivery with phased, zone-by-zone construction intended to preserve operations, with institutional-grade noise, dust and access management and weekly progress reports. Exact method and programme are confirmed per project — we do not publish a fixed method claim here.

Working with international teams

Do you work in English with a PM outside Taiwan?

Yes. We provide a bilingual project manager as a single local window, English proposals and meeting notes, and Chinese filings for Taiwan authorities and landlords. Revit / CAD / PDF handoffs are accepted; we produce the local permit and tender sets Taiwan authorities need.

How fast do you reply, and who is the single point of contact?

We target a first acknowledgement within one business day. Email info@hqdesign.tw or lily.chang@hqdesign.tw, call +886-2-2557-3003, or use the contact form. On Local Partner engagements, one bilingual PM is your single local contact.

How do listed-company or foreign-HQ procurement teams typically start?

Send layout or design intent (PDF / CAD / Revit as available), building and unit information, landlord handbook if you have it, target move-in, preferred engagement model (Design & Build, Local Partner / permit-only / build-only / full local execution), and the design PM contact to info@hqdesign.tw or via the contact form.

We acknowledge within one business day and propose next steps on an agreed short timetable. English for international stakeholders; Chinese for Taiwan filings.
