Engagement models

Design & Build vs Local Partner — which do we need?

Choose Design & Build when you want one turnkey contract for design and construction under one in-house team and one PM — from briefing through handover.

Choose Local Partner when an overseas or captive design studio keeps design authorship and the client relationship; HQ Design then handles Taiwan permits, compliance, procurement and site supervision. Many international design PMs and CRE teams use the Local Partner path. See Local Partner and Services.

Can our overseas design studio keep design authorship?

Yes. Under the Local Partner model we do not take over design authorship. Your studio keeps creative control and the client relationship. HQ Design produces local permit and tender drawings and runs Taiwan landlord and statutory coordination under your design lead.

Can we engage HQ for permits only while design stays with us?

Yes, after assessment. Permits and compliance can be purchased standalone even when another firm designs and builds. We also offer build-only and full local execution (permits, procurement and site) while you keep design lead. Tell us which model you need when you enquire.

Why a dedicated project manager, site manager, and designer?

Role separation keeps accountability clear. A dedicated project manager is your single client window for programme, budget dialogue, and change control; a site manager owns quality, safety, and trade coordination on site; a designer holds design intent and buildability. Together they prevent one person from carrying every trade, permit, and stakeholder alone.

This model matters most for multi-trade commercial fit-outs, landlord- and permit-heavy buildings, occupied or zero-downtime sites, and multi-stakeholder clients — not every tiny shop. Our About page lists the team that makes this staffing possible. See How we staff a project on Services.

Taiwan permits & delivery

Who handles Taiwan interior renovation, fire and landlord permits?

HQ Design coordinates interior renovation filings, related fire submissions, and landlord / building-management requirements as applicable to each project. Scope is assessed with the proposal — we do not publish a fixed always-in-scope permit matrix. Permit submissions are stamped by a registered interior-renovation professional on our team.

Can you support zero-downtime or occupied-building fit-outs?

Yes at a programme level. Our site positioning includes Zero Downtime / turnkey delivery with phased, zone-by-zone construction intended to preserve operations, with institutional-grade noise, dust and access management and weekly progress reports. Exact method and programme are confirmed per project — we do not publish a fixed method claim here.

For procurement & general affairs

What makes a design-build firm easier for procurement or general affairs to work with in Taipei?

For foreign HQ and listed-company fit-outs in Taiwan, the department that runs the vendor relationship is usually procurement or general affairs (admin). Lower coordination stress typically comes from a single accountable engagement path, named roles on one in-house team, bilingual commercial documents with Chinese for Taiwan filings, a stated reply window, and a clear owner for programme and change orders — rather than chasing separate trades.

HQ Design offers Design & Build (one turnkey contract under one PM) or Local Partner (your studio keeps design authorship; HQ handles Taiwan permits, compliance, procurement and site). Each project is staffed with a dedicated project manager, site manager, and designer. We target a first acknowledgement within one business day. Soft items such as permit scope stay assessment-required. See Services — For procurement & general affairs and what to send.

What reduces stress for procurement or general affairs during a Taiwan office fit-out?

Common stress points are unclear ownership of schedule and changes, bilingual document gaps, and slow first replies. HQ Design addresses those with: (1) one engagement model you choose — Design & Build or Local Partner; (2) role separation — dedicated PM as client window, site manager on site, designer on intent; (3) English commercial documents and Chinese regulatory filings; (4) first acknowledgement within one business day; (5) 12-month warranty after handover.

We do not publish a fixed always-in-scope permit matrix or site-survey SLA — those are confirmed per project after assessment. Staffing detail: How we staff a project.

One contract or many vendors — who owns schedule and change orders?

Under Design & Build, design and construction sit under one contract and one in-house team; the dedicated project manager is your client window for programme, budget dialogue, and change control. Under Local Partner, your design studio keeps authorship while HQ owns the Taiwan execution path it is engaged for (permits, compliance, procurement and/or site as scoped).

In both models the PM remains the single client window for schedule dialogue and change control on HQ’s scope, so procurement or general affairs is not left coordinating every trade alone. Compare models on Services and Local Partner.
