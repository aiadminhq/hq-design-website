Move-out and handover checklist

This is the order a move-out usually follows. The scope still depends on the lease, the landlord and that building’s management rules. You can print the list and take it to the survey or the handover.

  1. Review the make-good clause in the lease, and mark what must be demolished, what stays, and what must be repaired.
  2. Survey the office together with the landlord, and compare it with the condition when you moved in.
  3. Agree the demolition and repair scope, and write it down as one list.
  4. Complete the building’s works notice, or notify the management committee, following that building’s rules.
  5. Carry out the demolition. Mark the landlord’s property first, and start only after that is confirmed.
  6. Remove the demolition waste along the route and within the hours the building allows, and keep the disposal certificate.
  7. Patch, repaint, and complete any other repairs the lease requires.
  8. Clean the space so it is ready for handover.
  9. Inspect the space together with the landlord, item by item, and keep photos.
  10. Sign the handover confirmation, and keep a copy for each side.
  11. Ask for the deposit to be returned, according to the lease and the handover result.
