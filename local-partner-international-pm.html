Permit route

From lease check to handover

Ordered steps — no durations stated; programme is confirmed per project.

  1. Lease & landlord check — confirm unit rules, handbook requirements and submission gates
  2. Design-intent review — read your drawings for Taiwan code and landlord fit
  3. Local drawing set — produce the permit and tender drawings the authorities and builders need
  4. Permit filing — interior-renovation permit and related fire submissions
  5. Procurement & subcontracting — price and place local trades under the agreed model
  6. Site supervision — manage the works on the ground
  7. Completion inspection & handover — close out inspections and hand the space back cleanly
