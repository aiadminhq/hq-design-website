Office renovation inquiry

Office renovation, from space planning and design through fit-out and execution, with one accountable point of contact

When a company renovates or moves offices, the schedule and who is responsible come first. HQ Design assigns a project manager for progress and changes, a site manager for the works, and a designer for the design. Planning, design, fit-out, and execution sit in one arrangement.

  • Project manager — progress, changes, and the external point of contact.
  • Site manager — the works and the day-to-day site.
  • Designer — the design, and whether it can be built as drawn.

The work is split across these roles. It is not one person doing every trade, and it is not several contractors each answering on their own. The schedule is not set before the site survey.

What we handle

Space planning, design, fit-out, relocation, and reinstatement

The four items below can be done together, or only the part you need. Scope is confirmed after the site survey.

Space planning

Circulation, meeting rooms, open office, and reception, arranged around how the office is used.

Design

Requirements turned into a design that can be built, using the drawings you already have.

Fit-out

Construction to the confirmed scope, coordinated with that building's access rules.

Relocation and reinstatement

New-office fit-out aligned to move-in, and old-office reinstatement aligned to handover. This is the space work, not furniture or equipment transport.

How it proceeds

Five steps

Who is responsible at each step is written into the same arrangement after the site survey.

  1. Understand the brief

    Address, approximate size, target move-in or move-out, and whether drawings already exist.

  2. Site survey

    We look at the space, the lease scope, and the building rules. The schedule is set after the survey.

  3. Plan and quote

    A plan and a quotation based on the confirmed scope.

  4. Construction

    Work proceeds to the agreed scope. The project manager owns the schedule. The site manager owns the site.

  5. Handover

    Items are checked one by one, then the space is handed over or handed back.
