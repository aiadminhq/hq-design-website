Services

Six named services — from design-and-build to permits you can buy on their own.

Design and build in one contract

Design and construction by the same in-house team under one contract and one PM — from briefing through handover.

02

Permits and compliance

Interior-renovation permit filing, fire review, and completion inspection coordination. Can be purchased on its own — even when another firm designs and builds the space. We work under your design lead; we do not take over design authorship.

03

Construction management

Site supervision, programme control, quality and safety oversight for commercial fit-out in Taiwan.

04

Public-sector tender packages

Documentation and packaging support for public-sector procurement processes tied to interior fit-out delivery.

05

MEP coordination

Mechanical, electrical, and fire-protection coordination with the interior design so systems meet code and stay buildable.

Reinstatement and handback

End-of-lease reinstatement and structured handback so the landlord and next occupier receive a clear, compliant close-out.
