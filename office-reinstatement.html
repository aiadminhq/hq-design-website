Common Problems

Does any of this sound familiar?

Most people only move out of an office once every few years, so it's normal not to know how it works.

01

The lease is ending and you don't know where to start

Who to call, how long it takes, what to prepare: nobody gives you a clear answer, and the date keeps getting closer.

02

You're not sure what to remove and what belongs to the landlord

Ceilings, lights, air-conditioning: some were added by your company, some were there from the start. Remove the wrong thing and you pay for it; miss something and the handover fails.

03

You're worried about deductions from your security deposit

On handover day the landlord says “this part hasn't been reinstated”. The deposit is held back, you've already moved out, and it's hard to go back and fix it.

04

The building only allows works at night or at weekends

When works are allowed, how much noise is acceptable, how waste leaves the building: every building has its own rules.

Scope

What we do, and what we leave alone

In short: we remove what you installed, put back what the landlord asks for, and leave the landlord's property untouched. Leases written in English often call this a “make good” obligation. We take on any office, not only ones we fitted out.

What we do

  • Strip out partitions and fit-outPartition walls, glass partitions, cabinetry, carpet and signage, plus any wiring and equipment you added later.
  • Reinstate what the landlord specifiesWalls, floors or equipment the landlord wants put back, restored according to their list.
  • Licensed waste removalEverything we remove goes to a licensed waste contractor, and you receive a waste disposal certificate.
  • Final cleaningWhen the works are finished we clean the space so it is ready for handover.
  • Attend the handoverOn handover day we are on site to go through each item with you and the landlord, and fix any issues on the spot.

What we don't touch

  • The landlord's propertyAnything that belongs to the landlord and must stay. Typically ceilings, light fittings, air-conditioning fan coil units, and curtains or blinds.

Before works start, we mark the landlord's property on site and confirm it with you before anything is removed.

Not sure who owns something? We'll look at it together during the site survey, and we can check with the landlord for you.

What to Prepare

What you need to prepare

Send us what you have. We'll help you find the rest.

  • Photos of the space as it is nowPhone photos are fine, a few of each area.
  • Photos or handover records from when you moved inThese show what the space “originally” looked like.
  • The landlord's reinstatement list, or the reinstatement clause in your leaseWhat the landlord wants removed and what they want put back. If it was only agreed verbally, tell us that too.
  • Original drawingsAs-built drawings or interior fit-out drawings.
  • The building's rules for worksPermitted working hours, noise limits, and rules for waste removal and loading.

Don't have any of these? That's fine. We measure on site during the survey, and we can check with the landlord or building management for you.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Will I get my security deposit back?

Whether the deposit is returned is the landlord's decision, and we can't guarantee it on their behalf. What we can do is lower the risk of deductions: we work to the landlord's reinstatement list, keep before-and-after photos, and on handover day go through each item with you and the landlord, fixing any issues on the spot.

What if I don't know what needs to be removed?

That's fine. Most clients aren't sure at the start. Just choose “Not sure” on the form. During the site survey we'll go through each item with you on site, and we can also check with the landlord or building management for you.

Could the landlord's property be removed by mistake?

Before works start, we mark the landlord's property on site. Typical items are ceilings, light fittings, air-conditioning fan coil units, and curtains or blinds. Work only begins after you have confirmed what we marked.

Our building only allows works at night or at weekends. Is that OK?

Yes. Every building has its own rules on working hours, noise and waste removal. We confirm them with building management first, then schedule the works to fit.

What happens to the waste?

A licensed waste contractor takes it away for disposal. Afterwards we give you a waste disposal certificate, which you can keep or pass on to your landlord or company for their records.

What if we don't have the original drawings?

That's fine. Drawings make things faster, but without them we measure and photograph the space during the site survey.

How long does it take, and how is it priced?

It depends on the floor area, what needs to be removed, and the hours the building allows works. After the site survey we give you a clear schedule and a quote based on floor area and scope. The earlier you tell us your lease end date, the easier it is to schedule.

We have several offices to move out of. Can you handle them together?

Yes. The form lets you add several locations at once, and we'll plan all of them together around their lease end dates.
