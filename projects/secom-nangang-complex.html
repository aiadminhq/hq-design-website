Project Overview

One headquarters · six floors

SECOM’s Nangang headquarters was a single-tenant design & build fit-out. HQ Design planned and built six floors of workplace for SECOM, delivering more than 600 seats with phased, floor-by-floor construction so operations continued without disruption.

Delivery capabilities
