Corporate Office

中保南港大樓
SECOM Nangang Complex

台北市南港區經貿二路 完工 2025 Design & Build HQ Interior
企業辦公 · Corporate Office

中保集團南港辦公大樓多戶統包專案，單棟涵蓋多家子公司與進駐企業，分層分區同步施工，確保各租戶營運不中斷。惠強以完整設計施工整合能力，統籌規劃 9F–11F 共 6 個獨立辦公空間。

A multi-tenant design & build project within SECOM Group's Nangang headquarters tower. HQ managed simultaneous fit-outs across floors 9–11 for six independent tenants, delivering each space without disrupting the others.
