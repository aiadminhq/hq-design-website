Our Story

Precision craftsmanship.
Everything in place.

HQ Design CO., LTD. (惠強室內裝修股份有限公司) was founded in 1995. For three decades we have worked with clients to combine design thinking, strategy and context — turning commercial space into a place where companies, brands and people meet.

Founded in 1995, HQ has delivered over 1,600 commercial renovation projects across Taiwan. Our clients include listed corporations, international hotel brands, major financial institutions, chain restaurants, and government bodies, with public proof points including SECOM, Kimpton, Popeyes, Soup Spoon, Cheogajip, C.SUN (Linkou, New Taipei), AIONTECH, Goldsun Group, TransAsia Airways, and V Air.
